Research Experience

  • 2025–Present
    Research Assistant (Remote)
    Agentic Intelligence Lab, University of Hong Kong
    • Advisor: Prof. Jiayu Chen.
    • Task-free online continual learning: developed Streaming Subspace Routing (SSR), which uses frozen, unprompted ViT-B/16 feature geometry to route inputs among low-rank LoRA experts without task identity or class-based routing at inference.
    • Designed streaming subspace updates and soft expert composition from residual distances. Rank-8 LoRA experts adapt attention query and value projections while the backbone stays frozen.
    • Evaluated CIFAR-100, Tiny-ImageNet, and ImageNet-R under the Si-Blurry protocol with replay budgets of 0, 500, and 2,000 samples. Multi-seed results are reported as Aauc, Alast, and Flast.
    • Built a preceding shared-prompt method with slow-learning LoRA adapters and EMA-based logit distillation. At 500- and 2,000-sample replay it improved over SinglePrompt on all three datasets. ImageNet-R gains reached +6.62 Aauc and +7.19 Alast.
    • Continual robot learning: preliminary work on BEHAVIOR-1K household manipulation in OmniGibson and Isaac Sim, including task-stream design and demonstration-based learning.
    • Continual diffusion policies: sequential adaptation across Lift, Can, and Square in Robomimic, focusing on specialization, forward transfer, and distillation between stages.
  • 2024

    • Implemented, trained, and compared PPO, TD3, and SAC for Franka Panda assistive-arm motion planning in simulation.
