\section{Research Experience}
\cvhead{University of Hong Kong}{Research Assistant (Remote), Agentic Intelligence Lab --- Advisor: Prof.\ Jiayu Chen}{Hong Kong}{Sep 2025 -- Present}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Task-free online continual learning:} Developed \textit{Streaming Subspace Routing} (SSR), which uses frozen, unprompted ViT-B/16 feature geometry to route inputs among \textit{low-rank LoRA} experts without task identity or class-based routing at inference.
  \item Designed streaming subspace updates and soft expert composition using residual distances; applied rank-8 LoRA experts to attention query and value projections while keeping the backbone frozen.
  \item Evaluated CIFAR-100, Tiny-ImageNet, and ImageNet-R under the Si-Blurry protocol with replay budgets of 0, 500, and 2,000 samples; conducted multi-seed experiments and reported $A_{\mathrm{auc}}$, $A_{\mathrm{last}}$, and $F_{\mathrm{last}}$.
  \item Built a preceding \textit{shared-prompt} method with \textit{slow-learning LoRA} adapters and \textit{EMA-based logit distillation}. Across 500- and 2,000-sample replay settings, it improved over SinglePrompt on all three datasets; ImageNet-R gains reached $+6.62\,A_{\mathrm{auc}}$ and $+7.19\,A_{\mathrm{last}}$.
  \item \textbf{Continual robot learning:} Conducted preliminary research on BEHAVIOR-1K household-manipulation tasks using OmniGibson and Isaac Sim, including task-stream design and demonstration-based learning considerations.
  \item \textbf{Continual diffusion policies:} Investigated sequential adaptation across the Lift, Can, and Square manipulation tasks in Robomimic, focusing on specialization, forward transfer, and knowledge distillation between task stages.
\end{itemize}
