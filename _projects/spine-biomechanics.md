My B.Sc. thesis, advised by Prof. Navid Arjmand in the Spine Biomechanics Laboratory at Sharif University of Technology, studied ergonomic risk for workers performing seated tasks.

I collected workplace posture data, built biomechanical models in 3DSSPP and AnyBody, and quantified ergonomic and spinal-injury risk. A workstation-tilter intervention reduced model-estimated ergonomic risk by more than 50% and lowered the estimated L4–L5 compression force.

Related laboratory work at the Research Center of Intelligent Neuro-Rehabilitation collected in vivo kinematic and force data for gait and manual material handling.
