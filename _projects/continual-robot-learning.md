Alongside the vision continual-learning work, I have been studying how robot policies can be adapted across a sequence of manipulation tasks.

For household manipulation, this is preliminary work on BEHAVIOR-1K in OmniGibson and Isaac Sim. The focus so far is task-stream design and what demonstration-based learning requires in that setting.

For tabletop manipulation, I have been looking at continual diffusion policies across Lift, Can, and Square in Robomimic. The questions are specialization, forward transfer, and knowledge distillation between task stages.
