At Sharif University of Technology I implemented, trained, and compared PPO, TD3, and SAC for Franka Panda assistive-arm motion planning in simulation. Hyperparameters were tuned with Optuna, and I compared convergence under manual and optimized settings.

This work is reported in Naeimi Tabiei, Haji Zargarbashi, Madadi, and Ahmadian, “The Role of Deep Reinforcement Learning in Motion Planning for Robotic Arm Assistive Systems,” 33rd Annual International Conference of the Iranian Society of Mechanical Engineers, Tehran, 2025 ([Civilica: ISME33_085](https://en.civilica.com/doc/2463147/)).
